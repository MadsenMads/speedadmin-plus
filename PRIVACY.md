# Speedadmin Plus Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-10

Speedadmin Plus is a Chrome extension that adds week numbers and hour conversions to SpeedAdmin pages.

## Information handled

The extension handles the following information locally on the device:

- The enabled or disabled setting selected in the extension popup.
- Text already displayed on SpeedAdmin pages, solely to find the date and minute labels used by its features.

The extension does not request account credentials, payment information, contact information, or other personal details.

## Use of information

The enabled setting controls whether the SpeedAdmin page enhancements run. Page text is used only to provide the SpeedAdmin week-number and minute-to-hour conversion features.

## Storage, sharing, and transmission

The enabled setting is stored with Chrome's local extension storage. The extension does not transmit page content, URLs, browsing activity, or other information to a developer-controlled server or third party. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies, or third-party services.

The extension can be disabled with the checkbox in the popup. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage according to Chrome's extension storage behavior.

## Permissions

- `storage`: stores the enabled/disabled setting locally.
- SpeedAdmin page access: reads displayed SpeedAdmin dates and minute totals locally to add the extension's calculations.

## Changes

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
